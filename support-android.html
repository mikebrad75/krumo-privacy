Krumo Support for Android

Need a hand with Krumo on your Android phone or tablet? Get help with reading features, audio, Google Play purchases, or anything that is not working as expected. Ideas and feedback are welcome too.

Contact us

Parents and guardians can email Krumo Support at:

krumo.support@gmail.com

For a technical problem, please include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of what happened. If an error appears, include its wording.

Premium stories are still locked

If you subscribed to Krumo Premium through Google Play but cannot access premium stories:

  1. Open the Google Play Store and check that you are using the same Google Account used for the subscription.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Check that your Krumo subscription is active.
  3. With an internet connection available, close and reopen Krumo, then check your premium access again.

If access is still missing, contact us using the email above. If Krumo is missing from your Google Play subscriptions, check any other Google Accounts you use.

Google Play's help with in-app purchase problems.

Manage or cancel a Google Play subscription

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android device and tap your profile icon.
  2. Choose Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  3. Select Krumo to view your subscription. To stop renewal, tap Cancel subscription and follow the prompts.

Uninstalling Krumo does not cancel your subscription. After cancellation, you can continue using your subscription for the time you have already paid for.

You can also open your Google Play subscriptions online or read Google's subscription cancellation instructions.

Help with story audio

If you cannot hear the narration, check your device's media volume and whether sound is being sent to Bluetooth headphones or another connected speaker. Then try playing a story sentence again.

If the issue continues, tell us which story and Spanish audio variant you selected so we can investigate.
