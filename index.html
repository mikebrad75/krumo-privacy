Krumo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Krumo is a Spanish reading and story app designed for children and families. We take children's privacy seriously and aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information we collect

Krumo does not require children to create an account or provide personal information in order to read and interact with stories.

Krumo includes an optional early-access feature intended for parents or guardians. Access to the parent early-access form is protected by a parental gate.

If a parent or guardian chooses to use this feature, we collect:

  • A parent or guardian email address.
  • An optional response about interest in a possible future Krumo subscription price.

This information is submitted only when a parent or guardian chooses to provide it.

How we use this information

A submitted email address is used only to communicate about Krumo launch, early-access availability, and related early-access updates.

Optional pricing-interest responses may be used to help us understand interest in possible future Krumo subscription options.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use submitted email addresses for behavioral advertising.

Service providers

Early-access submissions are transmitted securely over the internet through a Google Apps Script service used by Krumo to receive and process those submissions. Google may process information as part of providing that infrastructure.

Information stored on the device

Krumo stores a small number of app preferences locally on the device, such as the selected Spanish audio variant and whether certain introductory guidance has already been shown. These preferences are used only to make the app work as expected and are not transmitted to Krumo as part of the early-access service.

Advertising and analytics

Krumo does not currently include third-party advertising or behavioral advertising. Krumo does not currently use third-party analytics services to track children or their activity.

Other device information

Krumo does not currently ask for or collect precise location, contacts, photos, camera recordings, microphone recordings, or similar personal device data.

Children's privacy

Krumo is intended for use by children with the involvement of parents or guardians. Children can use the story-reading features without providing personal information.

The optional area in which an email address may be submitted is intended for parents or guardians and is placed behind a parental gate.

Data retention and deletion

We keep parent or guardian early-access information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for Krumo launch, early-access communication, related product research, or applicable legal requirements.

A parent or guardian may request deletion of their submitted email address and associated early-access information at any time by contacting us at krumo.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy as Krumo changes. If we make material changes to how personal information is handled, this page will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: krumo.support@gmail.com
