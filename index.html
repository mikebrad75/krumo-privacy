Krumo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Krumo is a Spanish reading and story app designed for children and families, developed by Dragos Mihai Brad. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Krumo or contact us.

Information handled by the app

Krumo does not require an online account. We do not collect children's names, reading activity, or app preferences on our own servers. Krumo does not include an email signup or pricing-interest form.

Information stored on your device

Krumo stores information locally to personalize the reading experience and remember settings. This includes the name entered for the greeting, the selected Spanish audio variant, reading preferences, and whether introductory guidance has been shown. You can use a nickname for the greeting.

This information is not sent to us. Local app data can generally be removed by deleting the app and its data. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple or Google backup settings.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. The store handles your store account and payment information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Krumo uses purchase and subscription-status information supplied by the store to unlock premium stories, restore purchases, and determine whether access is active. The app does not send these transaction records to a server operated by us.

Access to the subscription purchase area is protected by a parental gate. Parents or guardians can manage subscriptions through their Apple or Google account. Deleting Krumo does not cancel a subscription or delete records held by the store.

  • Google Privacy Policy

Story audio and device permissions

Story narration is prerecorded. Using narration does not send a child's speech or name to a speech-generation service.

Krumo does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, the camera, or the microphone for its story-reading features. Tell the Story is a storytelling activity; it does not record or upload the child's voice.

Advertising and analytics

Krumo does not display third-party advertisements or use behavioral advertising. We do not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting services in the app to track users or their reading activity. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Apple and Google may separately process store, purchase, and diagnostic information under their own policies and your device or account settings.

Support and voluntary contact

If you email krumo.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Our support mailbox is hosted by Gmail, which processes messages as part of providing its email service.

Parents and guardians should contact us on behalf of children and avoid sending unnecessary personal information about a child.

Children's privacy

Krumo is designed for children with the involvement of parents or guardians. The app's locally stored greeting name and preferences are not transmitted to us. Purchases are intended for parents or guardians and are protected by the parental gate described above.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support or another direct communication, contact us so we can address the issue and delete the information where appropriate.

Retention and deletion

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve related issues, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have supplied to us by emailing krumo.support@gmail.com. This also applies to information supplied before this policy was updated. We may need to verify that the request comes from the person concerned or their parent or guardian.

Local app information remains on your device until it is changed or removed. Information held independently by Apple or Google is subject to their retention policies and account controls.

External links

Krumo includes links to its privacy policy and store subscription-management pages. When you open an external page, its provider may process technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. This privacy-policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Krumo changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. Material changes to how personal information is handled will be explained in the updated policy.
