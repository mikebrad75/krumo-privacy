Krumo Data Deletion

Krumo does not require users to create an account. The only personal information Krumo may collect is information voluntarily submitted by a parent or guardian through the early-access feature.

How to request deletion

  1. Email krumo.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Krumo data deletion request.
  3. Tell us the email address that was submitted to the Krumo early-access list. If possible, send the request from that same email address.

What we will delete

We will delete the parent or guardian email address submitted through the Krumo early-access feature and any associated pricing-interest response.

What is not stored by Krumo

App preferences such as the selected Spanish audio variant and whether introductory guidance has been shown are stored locally on the device. They are not associated with the early-access email information held by Krumo. Local app data can be removed by uninstalling Krumo or clearing the app's data on the device.

Deletion timeframe

We aim to complete valid deletion requests within 30 days. Information may be retained for longer only when required by applicable law, and only for the required period.

Contact

For questions about data deletion or privacy, contact krumo.support@gmail.com.
